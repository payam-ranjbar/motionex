## What worked (keep it)
- **Errors that name the field** (`/unit is not a known field`, now also in `detail`). Agents fix them in one step.
- **Measuring instead of listening.** These tools let agents (and Claude) finish a mix without ears:
  - per-bar `audio.levels` (is the drop the loudest bar?);
  - `audio.speech-ratio` (is the voice clear?);
  - `audio.loudness` on the encoded file;
  - frames from the final MP4.
- **Stills → contact sheet → full render → frames from the MP4.** Every episode caught real bugs this way:
  - frame 1 empty;
  - wrong bar arithmetic;
  - a burst landing before the drop;
  - shades landing on hidden faces.
- **Re-mux instead of re-render** for audio-only changes (`media.mux`, video stream copied): three of the user's EP7
  revisions took about 1 minute instead of about 5.
- **Pins** as the record of "the approved take", and a `notes.md` per piece.

## Craft insights (go into the skill)
1. **A landing is not an impact.** Shapes that *form* should complete on the beat. A burst, explosion or flash should
   *start* on the beat. The EP6 whale was finished 0.4 s before its drop.
2. **The story beat must be visible.** Draw the subject of the moment in front. The sunglasses landed on faces hidden
   behind the div; the user then asked for the div to be on top. Check faces and props at the exact frame.
3. **A cue lands when it is heard, not when its file starts.** Place a vocal or SFX clip early by its measured onset
   (`onset_s`, about 40-60 ms for a soft "h"), so it hits on the frame. The user heard the laugh as "after" the text.
4. **Match cue loudness by absolute speech-band level** (`speech-ratio` `voice_db`), not by ratio. Ratio depends on what
   the music is doing under each cue.
5. **The build must not be louder than the drop.** Distortion, drone and a snare roll made EP7's "threat" bars louder
   than its drop. Check per-bar LUFS; balance with per-bar `postgain(bars([...]))`.
6. **High glitter is loud to people.** Sparkles above 4 kHz with a sharp attack read as "too crisp and loud". Low-pass
   them, soften the attack, and keep them about 15 dB under the music.
7. **Vocal cues, not voice-over.** Shouts and counts work as instruments: short, treated (megaphone, doubling, hall),
   about +5 dB over the music in the speech band, and each used once (the user cut five "jaan"s down to two).
8. **User art on a glowing ground: cut it out** (`image.matte`), then add your own halo. Screen blending turned every
   frame pastel.
9. **Rule of three** for transformations (the user asked for a third div state). Give every state the same entrance
   language (the same flash, flare and sound).
10. **Deliver two files**: a high-quality master, and a `fit-size` copy for the platform (Telegram bot limit 50 MB).
    Copy the audio stream into the size-fitted file.
11. **Persian/RTL text in canvas**: Nastaliq from Google Fonts, `ctx.direction = 'rtl'`, wipe reveals right-to-left,
    no trailing punctuation.
