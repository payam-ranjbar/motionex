Made with motionex

ON THE ONE

Five vertical micro-stories where every cut, impact and word lands on the beat. Each one was made end to end by an AI coding agent from a one-page brief: it wrote the scene as an HTML page, composed the song in Strudel, synthesised the sound effects and the voice, mixed to −14 LUFS, checked its own stills, and rendered the MP4 through motionex.

Turn the sound on.

  • 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps
  • 25–28 s each
  • one agent per episode, run in parallel
  • 20–62 min and under $1 of model time each

The brief

A series bible and one page per episode: the beat map, the look, the word effect, the music sketch. The agents read the motionex skill first. The briefs are in the repo.

The tools

One Docker service with bare tools over HTTP: music.render, voice.piper, sfx.synth, audio.mix, picture.render and the analysis that lets an agent mix without ears.

The source

Every episode folder holds the agent's scene.html, song.strudel, SFX recipe, every job it submitted, and its production notes.
