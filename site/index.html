Made with motionex

ON THE ONE

Ten vertical micro-stories where every cut, impact and word lands on the beat. Each one was made end to end by an AI coding agent: it wrote the scene as an HTML page, composed the song in Strudel, built the sound effects and the voices, mixed to −14 LUFS, checked its own stills, and rendered the MP4 through motionex.

Turn the sound on.

  • 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps
  • 25–72 s each
  • scene in HTML · song in Strudel
  • mixed to −14 LUFS

Episodes 1–5 · one autonomous agent each

Five agents ran in parallel, each from a one-page brief: 20–62 minutes and under $1 of model time per episode.

Episodes 6–10 · with a director

Made by Claude with the same tools, working with a human director; episodes 8–10 went through rounds of review notes. The pictures grow from pure particles (6) to the director's own Persian-miniature characters (7) and generated transparent cut-outs animated in code (8–10). Episode 10 adds recorded CC0 sounds and gibberish voices.

The brief

A series bible and, for episodes 1–5, one page per episode: the beat map, the look, the word effect, the music sketch. The agents read the motionex skill first. The briefs are in the repo.

The tools

One Docker service with bare tools over HTTP: music.render, voice.piper, sfx.synth, audio.mix, picture.render and the analysis that lets an agent mix without ears.

The source

The folders of episodes 1–5 hold each agent's scene.html, song.strudel, SFX recipe, every job it submitted, and its production notes.
